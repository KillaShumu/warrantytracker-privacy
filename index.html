Warranty Tracker

A memory for everything you own.

Photograph a label or receipt and keep the product, its coverage, its documents and its repair history in one record on your phone.

  • Separate coverage layers: return windows, manufacturer and component warranties, protection plans, installation and repair warranties, credit-card benefits.
  • Every coverage fact carries a verification label and its sources.
  • Expiry reminders at 90, 30 and 7 days.
  • Receipts, manuals, invoices and photos kept as original files.
  • Service history and total cost of ownership.
  • Optional AI with your own Anthropic API key: label reading, receipt extraction, warranty research, troubleshooting and an assistant that answers from your records.
  • No account. Your records stay on your device.

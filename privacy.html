Warranty Tracker Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

This policy covers the Warranty Tracker app for Android ("Warranty Tracker", "the app"). The app is published on Google Play by Category5 ("we"). To reach us, use the developer contact details on the Warranty Tracker page in the Google Play Store.

The short version

Warranty Tracker keeps your ownership records on your phone. There is no Warranty Tracker account and no Warranty Tracker server: we never receive your products, receipts, photos, documents or questions. The app has no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

The AI features are optional. They work only after you enter your own Anthropic API key. When you use one, the app sends the specific photo, document or question you chose, directly from your phone to Anthropic's API over an encrypted connection, and shows you the answer. Without a key, every record-keeping feature still works and nothing leaves your phone.

Information we collect

None. We do not operate any server the app talks to, and we cannot see who uses the app or anything you store in it.

Information stored on your device

Everything you add is stored in the app's private storage on your phone: products and their identifiers (such as model and serial numbers), purchase details (retailer, date, price), coverage records (return periods, manufacturer warranties, protection plans, credit-card benefits), the sources and evidence behind them, documents and photos you add (receipts, invoices, manuals, labels), service and maintenance history, notes, alerts and an audit history of changes. Your Anthropic API key, if you add one, is also stored only on your device.

The original files you add are kept unchanged so they remain usable as evidence. Android protects the app's private storage from other apps.

Payment cards

To look up credit-card purchase benefits, the app asks only for the card issuer and the card's product name (for example "Chase" and "Amazon Prime Visa"). It never asks for a card number, security code, expiry date, bank login or any other financial credential. Please do not type such details into notes or other free-text fields.

Optional AI features and Anthropic

If you add your own Anthropic API key in Settings, you can use: product identification from a label photo or barcode, receipt and document reading, warranty and card-benefit research (which uses Anthropic's web search tool to find public sources), troubleshooting help and questions to the ownership assistant.

Each time you start one of these actions, the app sends only what that action needs, directly from your phone to Anthropic, PBC at api.anthropic.com over HTTPS: the photo or document you selected, the text you typed, and the relevant records for the product involved (for example its name, model, purchase date and coverage). For the assistant, it sends a summary of your stored records so that it can answer from them. The request is billed to your own Anthropic account and is handled under Anthropic's terms and privacy policy (anthropic.com/legal/privacy). We never receive or see these requests.

You can remove your key at any time in Settings, after which no AI request can be made.

Permissions

Camera: used only when you choose to photograph a product label or receipt, or scan a barcode. Pictures you take are saved in the app's private storage as part of the product's record. Notifications: used to remind you, on your device, before coverage expires and when registrations or maintenance are due. Internet: used only for the optional AI features described above and to open source links you tap.

Sharing and third parties

We do not share any data with anyone, because we do not have any. The only party the app ever sends your data to is Anthropic, and only when you start an AI action with your own key. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software.

Exporting and deleting your data

You can export everything, or a single product, as JSON, CSV or a PDF report from within the app. To delete everything, open Settings and choose "Delete all data", or uninstall the app. Either permanently removes your records, documents and settings from your phone. There is no copy anywhere else, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. See also how to delete your data.

Your records are excluded from Android cloud backup. Use the export feature if you want a copy elsewhere.

Accuracy

Coverage information shown by the app is organized from your own documents and from public sources, with a verification label on each item. It is not a guarantee of coverage: the manufacturer, retailer, plan provider or card issuer is the final authority on any claim.

Children

Warranty Tracker is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. As described above, the app does not collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at https://killashumu.github.io/warrantytracker-privacy/privacy.html with a new effective date. If a future version of the app handles data differently, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about your privacy: open the Warranty Tracker page in the Google Play Store and use the developer contact details shown there.

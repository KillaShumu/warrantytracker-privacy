Warranty Tracker

Delete your Warranty Tracker data

Warranty Tracker has no accounts and no server. Everything is stored on your phone, so you delete it on your phone. There is no copy held by us.

Delete everything

  1. Open Warranty Tracker.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Under Danger zone, choose Delete all data and confirm.

This permanently removes every product, document, photo, coverage record, service record, alert, setting and your saved Anthropic API key. Uninstalling the app has the same effect.

Delete one product

Open the product, choose Delete from its menu and confirm. Its documents and records are removed with it.

Requests sent to Anthropic

If you used the optional AI features with your own Anthropic API key, those requests were made from your own Anthropic account. Anthropic's handling and deletion options are described at anthropic.com/legal/privacy.
